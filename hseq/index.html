HSEQ (Health, Safety, Environment & Quality)

At Korea Atlantic Tank, the health and safety of our people, the protection of the environment, and the delivery of high-quality services are at the core of everything we do.

Our HSEQ standards reflect our dedication to operational excellence and sustainable growth, ensuring that we meet the needs of our stakeholders while upholding our corporate responsibilities.

Health and Safety

We prioritize the well-being of our employees, contractors, and clients by maintaining the highest safety standards across all our operations.

  • Zero-Incident Goal: We are committed to achieving zero workplace accidents through continuous training, robust safety protocols, and a culture of accountability.
  • Emergency Preparedness: Our facilities are equipped with state-of-the-art systems and procedures to respond effectively to emergencies.
  • Continuous Improvement: Regular audits and reviews ensure that our safety measures evolve to meet the latest industry standards.

Environment

Korea Atlantic Tanker is committed to minimizing our environmental footprint and contributing to a sustainable future.

  • Sustainable Operations: We implement energy-efficient technologies and eco-friendly practices to reduce emissions and waste.
  • Compliance: Our operations adhere to all local, national, and international environmental regulations.
  • Green Initiatives: We actively explore and adopt innovations that promote the use of renewable energy and sustainable resources.

Quality

Delivering premium services is integral to our mission. We focus on maintaining the highest quality standards to exceed client expectations.

  • ISO Certification: Our processes align with globally recognized quality management standards to ensure consistency and reliability.
  • Client-Centric Approach: We tailor our services to meet the unique needs of each client, fostering trust and long-term partnerships.
  • Innovation: By leveraging cutting-edge technology, we continuously enhance the efficiency and quality of our operations.

By unlocking the full potential of the earth’s resources, we help power economies, support industry, and fuel the ambitions of our clients — today, and for generations to come.

Our HSEQ Culture

  • Training & Awareness: Regular workshops and training programs empower our team to uphold HSEQ standards.
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Collaboration with clients, employees, and regulators ensures transparency and mutual trust.
  • Performance Monitoring: Advanced monitoring systems help us track and improve HSEQ performance metrics.

Our HSEQ Vision

To be an industry leader in health, safety, environmental stewardship, and quality management, setting benchmarks that inspire others in the tank storage sector.
